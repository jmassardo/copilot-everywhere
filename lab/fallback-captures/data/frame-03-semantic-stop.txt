Decision memo — NULL discount-rate semantics

KNOWN
7,113 orders have discount_rate IS NULL.
Some rows are consistent with zero discount; others may be missing records.

CANNOT BE INFERRED
No stored field records why discount_rate is NULL.
Correlations cannot classify every row as "no discount" or "not recorded."

DECISION OWNER
The team that owns the order-to-analytics pipeline.

PROPOSED DATA-CONTRACT CHANGE
Preserve provenance with an explicit discount status or reason.
Validate zero-discount values separately from missing values.
Approve any historical backfill policy before changing existing rows.

UNSAFE ANALYSES
- treating every NULL as zero
- treating every NULL as an unknown discount
- claiming row-level meaning from reconstructed estimates

BLOCKED — semantic contract and backfill policy require a human owner.
